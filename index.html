Grok Connection

Level → Hunter Rank Chart

Your Hunter Rank comes from your level (highest row whose min level you've reached). This is the rank mana readers detect. Defaults: E 1–19, D 20–39, C 40–69, B 70–109, A 110–159, S 160–229, National 230–299, Deity 300+.

Stat Rank Thresholds

Each stat gets its own rank, separate from your Hunter Rank (cap of rank n = 25×n + previous cap). Defaults: E 0–25, D 26–75, C 76–150, B 151–250, A 251–375, S 376–525, National 526–700, Deity 701+.
